About the firm

People decisions are business decisions.

Every hire shapes the culture, capability and future of an organisation. Yet too often recruitment is treated as a transaction rather than a strategy.

Baresic Advisory brings a considered, advisory-led approach to talent. We work closely with our clients to understand where the business is heading, define the people it needs to get there, and design the processes that attract, assess and secure them.

Beyond the hire, we advise on how to reward and motivate your workforce, so the talent you invest in stays engaged and performs at its best.

Specialist advice

Five core areas of expertise

Engage us across the full talent lifecycle, or for the specific capability you need most.

01

Organisational Recruitment Strategy

We align your hiring with your business objectives. From workforce planning and role design to sourcing channels and hiring governance, we build a recruitment strategy that delivers the capability your organisation needs, now and as it grows.

02

Assessment & Selection

Robust, fair and evidence-based. We design structured interviews, assessment frameworks and selection processes that look beyond the CV, so you identify the candidates with the capability, values and potential to succeed.

03

Talent Acquisition & Executive Search

Discreet, targeted search for senior and critical roles. We map the market, engage passive talent and manage the process end to end, presenting a considered shortlist of leaders ready to make an impact.

04

Recruitment Marketing

Great roles deserve great reach. We craft compelling job content, campaigns and candidate journeys across the right channels, attracting quality applicants and converting interest into engagement.

05

Employer Branding

Your reputation as an employer is a strategic asset. We help you define and articulate an authentic employee value proposition, so the right people want to join, and your existing people are proud to stay.
